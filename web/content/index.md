# cubes: a free online territory strategy game of light and corners

cubes is a free, open-source strategy game for two to four players that runs in any web browser. You place glowing shards (polyominoes in 2D, polycubes in 3D) on a shared board. Each new shard must touch your own light at a corner and may never touch it along a side. Whoever holds the most of the board when nobody can move wins.

There is nothing to install and no account to make. Send a friend a link and you are playing together in seconds, or play against three levels of bots right away.

## How it works

1. **Start at your Core.** Every player begins with one glowing Core cell. Your first shard has to touch it at a corner.
2. **Link at a corner.** Every later shard must touch your light at a corner, and never along a side. You may touch other players however you like.
3. **Go dark, then count.** When you have no legal move you go dark. When everyone is dark, you score one point per cell you hold, plus five for placing your whole kit.

## Ways to play

- **Plane:** the standard board, 20×20 for four players and 14×14 for two.
- **Duel:** two players on a small board, nowhere to hide.
- **Pure:** no nodes and no surges, just geometry.
- **Fog:** you only see two cells past your own light; everything else is memory.
- **Storm:** the edge of the board collapses into ash every few rounds.
- **Torus:** the board wraps around on both axes.
- **Teams:** two against two with shared light.
- **Stack (3D):** gravity, and you climb on other people's towers to crown columns.
- **Orbit (3D):** a floating cube where light spreads in every direction.

## Surges

Glowing nodes give charge. Spend it on a **Spark** (place a single cell), a **Leap** (link from two cells away), a **Shatter** (destroy one enemy cell) or a **Scan** (see through the fog).
