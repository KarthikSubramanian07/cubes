# A cubes room

This link is a live multiplayer room for cubes, a free territory strategy game played in the browser. Open it in a web browser to take a seat. Up to four people can play, empty seats can be filled with bots, and anyone else who opens the link watches.

Rooms need no account. The game remembers your seat if your connection drops, and the room is deleted 24 hours after its last activity.

- [How to play](https://playcubes.pages.dev/rules)
- [Make your own room](https://playcubes.pages.dev/play)
